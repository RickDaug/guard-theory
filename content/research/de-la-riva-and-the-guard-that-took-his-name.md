## 3. Contradictory accounts — how each was handled

- **B1 (year).** Not reconciled. All three dates from the same site are printed side by side and no date is asserted.
- **B2 (result).** Not reported at all. House rule: no competition result without a primary or federation record.
- **B3 (judo antecedent).** Reported as an assertion, with its evidence base named (video footage cited to a social media page). Neither endorsed nor denied.
- **B4 (first instructor).** Both accounts printed.
- **B5 (rank).** Both dropped; the article gives a period, not a rank.

## 6. Quotations approved

1. **Ricardo de la Riva**, in interview with Grapplearts: "We never gave it names back then and just used to call all of it the open guard." — verified verbatim from the page. This is the only direct quotation in the article and it is attributed to the interview in the sentence that carries it.
2. **BJJ Heroes**, feature: "This outside wrap is in essence what makes an open guard become a DLR Guard." — verified verbatim; paraphrased closely in the article with attribution.

**No quotation is attributed to any other person.**

## 8. Rights concerns

- **Living person.** Every biographical statement is attributed to a published source, and no motive, characteristic or private detail is imputed. The one quotation is his own words from an interview he gave.
- **A second living person, Royler Gracie**, appears only as a participant in a match whose result the article declines to state. This is deliberate: reporting a disputed 40-year-old result about a living competitor from a community wiki is exactly the kind of claim that should not be published.
- **Photography.** The BJJ Heroes feature credits its banner photograph to a named agency. Do not reuse.
- **Naming the researcher.** Bruno Schindler's research is described but he is not named in the article, because the claim attributed to him could not be verified and naming him would attach an unverified assertion to a real person.

## 9. Fact-check status

**Passed, with the following claims cut:**

- *Cut:* the date of the Copa Cantão match (three conflicting versions from one site).
- *Cut:* the result of that match, and the description of Royler Gracie as previously unbeaten. Both are competition-record claims with no primary source; "unbeaten" is doubly excluded by the house rule on undefeated claims.
- *Cut:* the 2002 World Championship bronze medal, the 2003 ADCC appearance, the 1993 retirement and the 2004 exhibitions. Competition and career claims from a community wiki only.
- *Cut:* the claim, from Grapplearts, that he beat Royler, Rolker and Royce Gracie, and Royce twice. Same reason.
- *Cut:* the attribution of the outside hook to Oda Tsunetane. Reported as an assertion; not stated as fact, and the name is not repeated.
- *Cut:* "voted as one of the top five technical fighters of all time by his peers" (Grapplearts). A ranking claim, excluded by the standing prohibition on rankings, and unsourced besides.
- *Cut:* the number of black belts Carlson Gracie promoted ("more then 100", Grapplearts). Unverifiable.
- *Cut:* both sources' characterisations of the position as an innovation that changed the sport, phrased in language the house style bans. The contribution is described in plain terms instead.

## 10. Editorial notes

- The article is structured so that the technical half stands on the rule book and the mechanics, and the historical half stands on named disagreement. If the history had to be removed entirely, the piece would still work. That is the correct dependency direction for a profile built on weak sources.
- Section 3 ("The record disagrees with itself") is the reason to publish this piece rather than another profile. It demonstrates the evidentiary standard on a case where the disagreement is visible on a single website, which is unusually easy for a reader to check for themselves.
- Do not add a "notable practitioners of this guard" list. BJJ Heroes has one; reproducing it is a ranking by another name.
- If a primary record of the Copa Cantão event is ever located — a results sheet, a contemporary newspaper report — this article should be updated and the correction noted, not silently edited.
