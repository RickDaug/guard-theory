## 3. Contradictory accounts and unresolved points

- **Rule book version number.** The `ibjjf.com/books-videos` page labels the download "IBJJF Rule Book (v6.0), June 2024". Every page footer inside the PDF reads "VERSION 6.1 2024". The article cites v6.1, matching the document itself. Recorded here so a later reader does not think it is an error.
- **The illegal-moves table.** The published table is a grid with six columns (4–12 years; 13–15; 16–17 and adult white belts; adult–Master 7 blue and purple; adult–Master 7 brown and black except adult no-gi; adult brown and black no-gi) and 26 numbered rows. `pdftotext` collapses the columns, so the row-to-column mapping **cannot be reconstructed reliably from the extracted text.** The article therefore rests its specific statements about adult brown/black no-gi permissions on the IBJJF's 2021 announcement plus two independent reports, and this limitation is stated in `contestedNotes`. **Anyone revising this article must read the table from the rendered PDF, not from extracted text.**
- **ADCC ruleset provenance.** The ADCC rules are published as an undated, unversioned web page. There is no way to cite "the rules in force at the 2022 event" from it. This is stated in the article body and in `contestedNotes`, and it is the reason the article makes no claim about any past ADCC edition.
- **Causation.** The central thesis — that pricing shapes technique selection — is an inference from incentives plus observation, not a measured finding. No public dataset of technique frequency by ruleset was located. Stated as inference in `contestedNotes`.
