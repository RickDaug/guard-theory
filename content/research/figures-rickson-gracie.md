## 2. Internal contradiction found and reported

BJJ Heroes states the first Rei Zulu fight took place in 1980 and that "Rickson was 18 years old at the time". A man born on 20 November 1958 turned 21 in November 1980. The two statements cannot both be true. Neither is sourced on the page. **Reported in `contestedNotes`; neither figure is asserted in the body.**

## 5. Claims cut

- *Cut:* the "400-0" figure as a record. Reported only as a myth, with the contradicting sambo loss.
- *Cut:* the result and conduct of the Yoji Anjo fight. Described only in accounts sympathetic to one side; footage never released.
- *Cut:* the quotation attributed to Rickson about fighting for money versus honour. It appears on the BJJ Heroes page without an interview citation and reads as folklore.
- *Cut:* the account of Carlos Gracie warning him off the first Zulu fight on spiritual grounds. Private, unverifiable, and about a third party.
- *Cut:* his Parkinson's disease diagnosis, reported by Sherdog news in 2023. It is public and it is sourced, but it is a health matter with no bearing on the contribution being recorded, and this index does not carry it.
- *Cut:* the "unbeaten in BJJ competition" claim on the BJJ Heroes achievements list, which the same page's caveat about incomplete records undermines.

## 6. Editorial notes

- The point of this entry is the gap between eleven documented wins and a claimed four hundred, and what that gap has done to how people argue about jiu-jitsu. Do not rewrite it as a debunking; the documented record is genuinely strong.
- Sherdog's fighter IDs are not guessable. Rickson is `/fighter/Rickson-Gracie-292`; `/fighter/Rickson-Gracie-233` is a different person entirely (Steve Judson) and was hit during research.
