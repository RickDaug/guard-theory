# Image production plan

Every shot the real photoshoot needs, specified so it can be booked and
executed without a further round of decisions.

Nothing here has been shot. The site currently uses drawn production flats
instead, which is a design decision rather than a placeholder — see
`visual-identity.md`. Photography will sit **alongside** the flats, not replace
them.

---

## Rules that apply to every shot

- **Everyone photographed consents in writing and is credited.** No exceptions,
  including for anyone who trains at the same gym as the photographer.
- **Nothing implies a sponsorship that does not exist.** No athlete is shown in
  a way that suggests they represent the brand unless they do.
- **No borrowed imagery.** Nothing scraped, nothing from a competitor, no stock
  of somebody else's gym, no generated photorealistic garment.
- **Bodies of more than one shape and more than one gender**, at more than one
  belt rank. A rash guard fits differently on different people and the
  photography should show that rather than hide it.
- **The room looks like a room people train in.** Not a studio pretending to be
  a gym, and not a gym dressed to look like a studio.
- If AI is ever used to generate any image on this site, it is **disclosed on
  the image**. Currently none is.

## Technical requirements

- Shoot RAW, deliver 16-bit TIFF masters plus web-ready AVIF and WebP.
- Minimum 3000px on the long edge for anything appearing on a product page.
- Colour-managed: sRGB for web delivery, with a grey card in the first frame of
  every setup.
- **Descriptive filenames**, not camera defaults:
  `theory-01-long-sleeve-front-flat.jpg`, not `DSC_04127.jpg`. This is an SEO
  requirement, not housekeeping.
- Every delivered image needs **functional alt text** written by whoever knows
  what the image is for — describing what matters about it, not listing what is
  in it. "Long sleeve rash guard, hem sitting below the waistband while the
  wearer's arms are overhead" beats "man in black rash guard".

## Sequencing

1. **A1–A9** the moment production samples exist. These unblock the product
   pages and can be shot on a table.
2. **B1–B5 ×3 wearers** immediately after, same day if possible, while the
   samples are pristine. These unblock the size guide, which is currently the
   largest honesty gap on the site.
3. **C1–C5** once the garments are in real use. These are the only shots that
   cannot be rushed, because posed grappling reads as posed.
4. **D1, D3** with final packaging. **D2** only when accessories are real.

## What this does not cover

Video. There is no video anywhere on the site and no plan for it yet. If that
changes it needs its own document rather than an appendix here.
