## 3. Contradictions, and how each was handled

- **When the ADCC guard-pull minus applies.** The ADCC page's time limits say qualifying rounds have "no points" in the first half and finals have "only negative points" counted in the first half. FloGrappling (2019) says pulling guard is penalised "at any time" and that there are no *positive* points in the first half. Both are printed in the article; the page as read today is followed; the possibility that they describe different versions is stated. Note that the companion article was corrected on 29 September 2026 on exactly this point, in the direction of the page.
- **IBJJF, ADCC and UWW on the pull itself.** Free with a grip (IBJJF); a minus (ADCC); two points to the opponent (UWW). Not reconciled. This is the subject of sections 5 and 6.
- **IBJJF, ADCC and UWW on the takedown.** Two points (IBJJF); two or four by finishing position (ADCC); two or three by amplitude, plus dominant-position points, plus a possible two for speed (UWW). Printed as a difference.
- **Sitting without contact.** IBJJF: serious foul, priced by the 7.2.1 ladder (nothing to the opponent on a first offence). UWW: caution and one point to the opponent at once. ADCC: no grip or contact condition found; the minus is the same either way.
- **IBJJF version label.** The books-videos page labels the link v6.0; the PDF footer reads VERSION 6.1 2024. Cited as the PDF reads, matching the existing entries.
- **UWW edition.** The copy on UWW's server is dated January 2021. `submission-only-and-the-overtime-problem` already notes that later editions circulate on national federation sites. Recorded in `contestedNotes`.

The conclusion that a pull is free of a minus in the first half of a qualifying round is a reading of three printed lines, set against a 2019 source that says otherwise.
