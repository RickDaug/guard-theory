## 0. The brief's hard constraint, and how it was met

The commission forbade describing any fighter as exclusively BJJ-based and forbade stating any fight result, record or statistic that could not be verified from a primary or reputable secondary source.

**The article names no fighter, describes no bout, and states no result, record or individual statistic of any kind.** Every claim in it is either a reading of a rule document or a sample-level median from one published performance analysis. §7 of the article states explicitly that describing anyone as a grappler who happens to be in a cage would be a claim about their preparation the article has no basis to make.

## 3. Contradictory accounts — how each was handled

- **Glove weight and cage dimensions are not in the ABC document at all.** A summariser asserted, from the California CSAC PDF, that gloves must weigh 4 oz and the enclosure is a 30-foot octagon with a 5-foot fence. **Both were fabrications.** The CSAC PDF is a verbatim copy of the ABC text and contains neither figure; I extracted it and grepped for "ounce", "cage", "diameter" and "feet" to confirm. Nevada's code was then used instead, and the article and `contestedNotes` both say these figures are Nevada's rather than universal. **This is the single most important entry in this file: had it not been checked, the article would have carried two invented specifications.**
- **UFC page vs ABC document.** The UFC's published foul list still includes the downward elbow. The ABC's current document does not. Not reconciled; the UFC page is not cited, and the discrepancy is noted here as an illustration of why secondary rule summaries were rejected.
- **12-6 elbow removal.** Two independent secondary reports agree on the vote and the 1 November 2024 date, and the two primary documents show the change. Treated as settled.
- **Miarka medians.** Two retrievals of the same abstract returned identical figures. Used, hedged as sample medians with observer-dependent coding.

## 6. Quotations approved

No quotation marks appear in the article, but four passages paraphrase the rules closely enough that the source wording was verified verbatim and is reproduced here for checking:

1. ABC, Standing up or Breaking Fighters: "The Referee shall either stand up or break the fighters when neither fighter is able to or fails to demonstrate real, significant and/or sustained effort to advance towards finishing the fight by any method. Simply maintaining what may be perceived as a superior position will not be considered effort to advance towards finishing the fight nor grounds for a guaranteed opportunity to maintain that position."
2. ABC, Dominance ¶3: "Merely holding a dominant position(s) shall not be a primary factor in assessing dominance. What the fighter does with those positions is what must be assessed."
3. ABC foul 12(a), 2025: "A fighter shall be considered grounded and may not be legally kneed or kicked to the head when any part of their body other than their hands or feet is in contact with the canvas (ground)."
4. ABC foul 12(a), 2022: "Any part of the body, other sole of the feet touching the fighting area floor. To be grounded, the palm of one hand (a flat palm) must be down, and/or any other body part must be touching the fighting area floor." (The typo in the source is reproduced here, not in the article.)

## 9. Fact-check status

**Passed, with the following claims cut:**

- *Cut:* **glove weight of 4 oz and a 30-foot octagon with a 5-foot fence**, both supplied by a summariser and both absent from the document it claimed to be reading. Replaced with Nevada's actual figures (4–8 oz; 20–32 ft) and flagged as jurisdiction-specific.
- *Cut:* the entire example of a well-known 2009 disqualification arising from the 12-6 elbow rule, including the fighters involved. It is the most colourful thing in the source material and it is a fight result about named people. Removed under the brief.
- *Cut:* any claim about how often takedowns succeed, how often fights reach the ground, or what proportion of finishes are submissions. Every figure I found for these traced to aggregator sites without stated methodology.
- *Cut:* the claim that fighters restart standing at the start of each round. It is almost certainly true in practice and it is **not** in either rule document. Replaced with the narrower statement that the rules provide a one-minute rest between rounds, so a position that exists at the bell does not exist sixty seconds later.
- *Cut:* any assertion that gloves prevent particular grips or make particular submissions harder. Widely repeated; no source found that measures it.
- *Cut:* any characterisation of what "wall wrestling" is or how it developed. The rules support the statement that the fence is a usable surface and that grabbing it is a foul; they do not support a history of the technique, and no citable history was found.
- *Cut:* the IBJJF ruleset as the grappling comparator. The rule book PDF could not be retrieved from `ibjjf.com` in this pass (the download link is JavaScript-driven and four URL patterns 404'd), and third-party mirrors were rejected. ADCC and the Spanias sample were used instead. **Reinstate the IBJJF comparison only from the primary PDF.**

## 10. Editorial notes

- The article's method is its integrity: five named constraints, each traced to a clause. If a section is cut for length, cut a whole constraint rather than thinning the citations.
- §7 is not a disclaimer, it is the argument's boundary. It states that the piece shows a difference in rules and not a cause of outcomes. Removing it would convert a rules analysis into a claim about fighters, which is exactly what the brief forbade.
- The two-document comparison in §3 (2022 vs 2025) is reproducible in five minutes by anyone with both PDFs, and that is the point. Keep the URLs to both versions in `sources` even though only one is "current".
- **Re-verify before publication.** The ABC amends this document most years, and a further amendment would silently falsify §3.
- If a future article covers the same ground, it must not introduce named fighters without re-opening the question this file settled.
