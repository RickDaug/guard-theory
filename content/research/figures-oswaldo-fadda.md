## 3. Image — REJECTED, no portrait used

Wikimedia Commons has exactly one candidate: **File:Osvaldo Fadda.jpg**.

- Licence: `{{self|cc-by-sa-3.0}}`, uploaded 2014-04-02 by user `Granduque1953`, source `{{own}}`.
- Description is only `{{Pt|Mestres de jiu-jitsu}}` — "jiu-jitsu masters". **The file page does not identify the sitter as Fadda.**
- The file was downloaded and inspected (196 × 257 px). It is **a painted portrait**, not a photograph, of an elderly man in a light gi.
- Fadda died in April 2005. A 2014 "own work" claim over a portrait of him is either a painting of the uploader's own making (in which case it is an artwork and its subject identification is unverified) or a scan of somebody else's work (in which case the licence is invalid).

**Verdict: not usable.** `image` is left `undefined`. This is the single most important gap in the set, because the under-documentation of the non-Gracie line is precisely the entry's subject.

**If a portrait is wanted later:** the Fadda family academy and Nova Uniao are the plausible rights holders; a direct permission request with a written CC licence grant, filed with Wikimedia VRT, is the correct route. Do not substitute a press or social-media photograph.

## 5. Editorial notes

- The strongest single piece of evidence in this entry is a rival conceding the point in print in 1955. It carries more weight than the challenge-match folklore and the entry orders it that way.
- Drysdale's position is genuinely destabilising for both lineages, not just this one, and the entry says so in its last paragraph. Do not soften that sentence into a defence of either family.
