## 4. Numbers that do not agree

UFC.com gives 13-2-2. Sherdog lists fifteen wins, because it counts bouts outside the UFC including a 2016 Bellator match against Ken Shamrock. Neither is wrong. The entry reports both and explains the difference rather than picking one.

## 5. Claims CUT

- **Cut: the 2007 doping allegation.** BJJ Heroes states that Royce Gracie tested positive for nandrolone after his 2 June 2007 win over Kazushi Sakuraba at Dynamite!! USA, that he disputed the California State Athletic Commission's finding, and that the result stayed on his record. A search located an apparently corroborating New York Times report of 3 July 2007 ("Drug Testing Hasn't Grown With a Sport"), but **nytimes.com is not retrievable from this environment (HTTP 403 to curl, refused by WebFetch)**, and no other accessible primary or established-news source was found. A single community-reference source is not enough to publish a doping allegation about a living person. The claim is cut from the body and the cut is recorded in `contestedNotes`.
  **Reinstate only** with a retrievable primary record (CSAC minutes or licensing action) or an established news report, and include his response.
- *Cut:* the UFC 60 date. Two sources give 26 and 27 May 2006. The year and the result are stated; the date is not.
- *Cut:* the claim that the early UFC proved jiu-jitsu superior to other styles. The entry explicitly narrows this to what the tournaments actually demonstrated.
- *Cut:* the "changed the face of combat sports forever" framing from the source.
- *Cut:* his return to a navy blue belt after Helio's death, and the associated claim about pre-federation Gracie belt colours. Single-source and not load-bearing.
