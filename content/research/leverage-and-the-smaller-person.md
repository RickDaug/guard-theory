## 3. Contradictions and wrinkles

- **Two words for one division.** The IBJJF rule book says "absolute" in 4.4.1 and "open class" in 5.9; the weight list prints "OPEN CLASS / ABSOLUTO"; ADCC prints "Absolute (Open) Class". The article reports this and treats them as the same thing.
- **The IBJJF rule book prints no weight limits.** It refers to "the weight limit of the division" (2.3.2) but the figures live on separate weight lists linked from event pages. The article therefore cites the list linked from one 2026 event and says so in `contestedNotes`.
- **Gi figures differ.** S7 quotes IBJJF gi limits in kilograms (nine male, eight female categories) from an older rule book. The article uses only the no-gi list read today and does not mix the two.
- **ADCC's female column for the World Championship shows no absolute line** in the image; the article attributes the "Absolute (Open) Class" line to the men's column only.
- **NJ 24A.1 as printed has an overlap** (light heavyweight to 204.9, heavyweight from 204). Only the first and last classes are reported.
- **S7's level comparisons are not size comparisons.** The article says this in the sentence after the finding.
