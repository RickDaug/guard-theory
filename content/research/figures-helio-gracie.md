# Research file — figures-helio-gracie

**Type:** Figures index entry
**All sources consulted:** 2026-08-04
**Fact-check status:** Passed. This is the highest-contradiction entry in the set; three sources give three different accounts of the same fight and all three are reported.

---

## 1. Claims made in the entry

### Treated as agreed

| # | Claim | Support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Born 1 October 1913, seventh of Gastao Gracie's nine children. | BJJ Heroes (Helio page and Carlos page birth-order list agree: Helio 1913). |
| A2 | Not permitted to spar as a child; admitted to classes after training in secret. | BJJ Heroes. |
| A3 | First vale tudo match in 1932 against the boxer Antonio Portugal, at 18. | BJJ Heroes biography and fight table (1932, Antonio Portugal, win). |
| A4 | Fought Masahiko Kimura in Rio on 23 October 1951 and lost. | BJJ Heroes (header gives the date); Judo Info Kimura biography (1951, Brazil). |
| A5 | Fought Waldemar Santana in 1955, lost, and the match is recorded at 3h42m. | BJJ Heroes. |
| A6 | Died 29 January 2009, aged 95. | BJJ Heroes. |
| A7 | The first Rio federation was created in 1967 with Helio as president, Carlson Gracie as director of the technical cabinet and Oswaldo Fadda as his deputy; matches were 5 minutes with 3 of overtime, takedowns and the mount worth 1 point. | BJJ Heroes (Fadda page) **and** GracieMag (Fadda page), independently. |
| A8 | In 1955 Helio told *Revista dos Esportes* that there had to be a Fadda, to show jiu-jitsu was not a Gracie privilege. | GracieMag, quoting the magazine. Rendered in the entry as reported speech, not as a verbatim quotation, because the original is Portuguese and no scan was seen. |

### Treated as contested

| # | Claim | Positions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B1 | The Kimura fight's finish. | BJJ Heroes: submitted by reverse ude-garami early in round 2. Judo Info (Jim Chen): elbow broken, Helio refused to submit, corner threw in the towel, TKO. Judo Info also points to an interview in which Helio said Kimura choked him unconscious. |
| B2 | The preliminary Japanese opponent. | BJJ Heroes: Yukio Kato, 22, ~70kg; first fight a draw, second a Helio win by cross choke. Judo Info: "Kado", choked unconscious by Helio after ~10 minutes. |
| B3 | Crowd and dignitaries. | Judo Info: 20,000 spectators, president and vice president of Brazil at ringside. BJJ Heroes gives no figure. Not asserted in the entry. |
| B4 | The 3-minute concession and the 36kg weight difference. | Both widely repeated; BJJ Heroes reports finding no record of the first and estimates the real difference nearer 10kg, noting neither man was weighed. |
| B5 | The "leverage" adaptation narrative. | Universal in family literature; Pedreira treats it as marketing. No source documents a specific technical change. |
| B6 | Federation year. | 1967 per BJJ Heroes (Fadda page) and GracieMag; 1969 per BJJ Heroes (Royce page). The two-source date is used and the conflict is recorded. |

## 4. Claims cut

- *Cut:* the "3 minutes" concession story. BJJ Heroes could find no record of it.
- *Cut:* the 36kg weight difference. Contradicted by the same source's own reasoning.
- *Cut:* the 20,000 crowd and the presidential attendance. Single source.
- *Cut:* "founder of Brazilian jiu-jitsu" and the source's closing claim that without him BJJ would have gone a different way. Unfalsifiable and hagiographic.
- *Cut:* his complete fight record as a tally. BJJ Heroes shows 8 wins and 2 losses in a table that plainly does not cover everything.

## 5. Editorial notes

- The three-way disagreement about the Kimura fight is the most valuable thing in this entry. Do not tidy it into one version.
- The 1967 federation detail matters because it undercuts the usual framing of Helio as purely anti-competition: he presided over the body that wrote the first points table.
