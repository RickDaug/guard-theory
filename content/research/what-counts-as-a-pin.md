## 3. Contradictions and how handled

- **Judo scoring changes between editions.** The 14 August 2026 SOR scores a hold-down at three levels including yuko (5-9 seconds). Earlier editions were not read today, so the article makes no statement about what they said; `contestedNotes` records that this is the August 2026 edition and that the file's own version history names 21 January and 24 July 2026 editions before it.
- **UWW Article 34 wording.** The sentence on the instantaneous fall reads "both shoulders of the attacking wrestler touching simultaneously and instantaneously the mat", which appears to describe the attacker's shoulders. A paraphrase was drafted and then cut for length; nothing in the article depends on it.
- **"Pin" in wrestling.** The UWW foreword uses "pin" (in quotation marks) and Article 35 uses "pinned"; the defining article uses "fall". The article reports both words where they occur.
- **Mate and standing up.** Article 11 says mate stops the contest temporarily; it lists specific cases in which athletes "must quickly return" to starting positions, and the ne-waza no-progress case is not in that list. The article therefore says the referee stops the contest and does not say the athletes are stood up.
- **Library versus rule books.** The Library's positional-hierarchy entry ranks side control above knee on belly as a pin and says the pin lives in the hips; the rule documents test the back and shoulders. Presented as two different jobs (what a referee can see, what does the holding), not as a disagreement.

## 5. Claims cut, and why

- *Cut:* "earlier editions scored hold-downs differently". Not read today; replaced with a statement that earlier editions were not read.
- *Cut:* that the referee "stands the athletes up" after mate for no progress in ne-waza. Not stated for that case (see section 3).
- *Cut:* UWW Article 43 as "one of two results that override the points gap". A misreading of the clause; reworded to what it supports.
- *Cut:* that a bridge in wrestling "has already cost points". Inference; reworded to say the rules pay the attacker for producing the danger position (Article 38).
- *Cut for length (verified):* IJF Article 17 on changing between hold-downs without the clock stopping; the golden-score rule (five seconds of osaekomi ends the contest with yuko); UWW Article 34 on the instantaneous fall; UWW Article 42's last paragraph on a wrestler in the danger position who shouts or taps; UWW Article 41 classification points; a paragraph on turning face down (UWW Article 35 against IBJJF 4.5).
- *Cut:* that the ADCC page never uses the word pin. True of the text read, but the page is HTML with script content and the claim added little.
- *Not stated:* any history of the rules, any Kodokan definition of osaekomi, any folkstyle or collegiate wrestling rule (not read), any claim about what newcomers typically expect beyond what the rules they trained under say.
