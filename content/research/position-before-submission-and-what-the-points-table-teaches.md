## 3. Contradictions and how each was handled

- **IBJJF version label.** The books-videos page labels the link "Rule Book (v6.0)"; the PDF it serves has "VERSION 6.1 2024" in the footer and "JUN.2024" on the cover. The source entry describes the PDF as read, matching how the existing entries cite it.
- **The two tables disagree on mount (4 against 2), on the back (4 against 3), on whether a pass-to-mount chain is paid twice, and on reversals.** Not reconciled. The disagreement is the subject of section 4 and the first point of section 6.
- **Knee on belly against side control.** Kesting ranks knee mount above side mount. The Library treats knee on belly as the lighter pin. Both tables pay for the knee and not for the side. The body reports all three and offers the site's reading as a reading. In `contestedNotes`.
- **Kesting's claim that the points system reflects the hierarchy.** Tested, not endorsed or rejected outright: the tables climb in the same direction and print nothing for the five rows underneath.
- **ADCC wording.** The reversal line on the page reads "reverse the position from top to bottom", which is evidently bottom to top. The article paraphrases ("reverses the position") and does not quote that phrase.
- **Wording of the saying.** "Position before submission" (Roadmap, the Library), "position comes before submission" (Roadmap, as quoted speech), "position over submission" (Grapplearts post, BJJ Mental Models). The body notes both wordings.

## 5. Consistency with the Technique Library

- `positional-hierarchy`: ranks by whose hips are free; back, mount, side control, knee on belly as "a lighter version of the same pin", half guard, guard as "the floor of the ladder", leg entanglements outside the ladder; "some value mount and the back equally"; "check the ruleset you compete under before trusting any number". The article uses each of these as stated and adds the specific numbers the entry deliberately leaves out.
- `mount-is-a-hip-pin`: "the pin lives in the hips"; a knee back inside means "the mount has become half guard with the name unchanged". Used in section 6 to show what a referee's criteria do not capture.
- The Library's advice to "escape to the position the escape can actually reach" is cited in section 6 and not altered.
