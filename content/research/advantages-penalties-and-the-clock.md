## 3. Contradictions and oddities

- **UWW Article 23 contradicts itself on cadets.** "For Cadets, Juniors and Seniors: the duration of a bout will be one period of 5 minutes", then the scoreboard countdown runs "from 4 to 0 minute for U15, cadets and veterans". The article follows the first sentence; `contestedNotes` 4 records it.
- **FloGrappling 2019 versus the ADCC page today.** Flo says there are "no positive points in the first half"; the page says qualifying rounds have "no points" in the first half and finals have no positive points. Already handled in `takedown-or-guard-pull` and not re-argued. The one Flo statement used here (negative points counted in at the end) is not on the ADCC page at all, and the body says so.
- **IBJJF 7.2.1 marks the fourth penalty with an asterisk** ("Disqualification of perpetrating athlete.*") and the extracted text of that page has no matching footnote. Nothing is claimed about it.
- **IBJJF 7.2.2** refers to "the escalating penalty sequence addressed in clause 7.3.1", although the sequence is printed at 7.2.1. The article cites 7.2.1 for the ladder.
- **IBJJF "Obs" under 1.1.3** contains a typo ("will are final"). Paraphrased.
