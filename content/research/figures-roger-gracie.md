## 2. The title-count problem

- rogergracie.com: "10 time Jiu-Jitsu world champion".
- BJJ Heroes lists world championship wins in 2004, 2005, 2006, 2007 (weight **and** absolute), 2008, 2009 (weight and absolute), 2010 (weight and absolute) — and separately refers in its prose to winning "the Mundials … for a record 14th time".

These are the same career counted two ways: whether a weight-division win and an absolute win at the same championship count as one title or two, and whether coloured-belt titles are included. **No total is asserted in the entry.** The specific years and the ADCC double are stated; the arithmetic is flagged.

## 6. Editorial notes

- The argument here is technical, not celebratory: a very small repertoire, taken to unusual depth, beating wider repertoires at the top of the ruleset. If the entry is revised, that has to remain the spine of it.
- The last paragraph on counting conventions is doing real work. It is the honest answer to the first question a reader will have, which is "so how many world titles?"
