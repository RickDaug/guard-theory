## 2. What is deliberately NOT claimed

- **No "first woman" claim.** Women competed in and won Brazilian jiu-jitsu and ADCC events before her; the ADCC women's divisions predate her first title. No source consulted supports calling her the first at anything, and the entry says so in its own text.
- **No technical innovation.** Her listed favourite position is the closed guard, which is as old as the sport.
- The claim about the state of women's divisions in the 2000s (small, poorly promoted, unfunded) is **contextual framing by this publication**, not a sourced statement, and the `contestedNotes` do not dress it up as one. It is the one interpretive move in the entry; if an editor wants it removed, the entry survives without it.
