## 3. Contradictory accounts — how each was handled

- **The two rulesets price the seated posture differently and on different theories.** IBJJF penalises sitting *without a grip* (a connection rule); ADCC penalises going to a non-standing position at all (a posture rule). Not reconciled — the difference is the point of that section, and the article tells the reader to check which applies at their event.
- **Terminology.** Seated guard, sitting guard and butterfly guard are used interchangeably by different schools, and no governing body defines any of them. The article defines its two terms explicitly, states that the definitions are ours, and says other schools split them differently.
- **Andreato internal discrepancy** (3 s vs 4 s): avoided by not stating a number.
