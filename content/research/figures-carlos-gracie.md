### Treated as contested

| # | Claim | Positions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B1 | Whether Maeda taught him personally. | Carlos's own recollection (via Cairus, given 65 years later); Cairus reports Maeda *and* Ferro; Drysdale points at Ferro; Pedreira: "None of them met him, with the possible but by no means certain exception of Carlos, who was an unreliable source of information about virtually anything and everything." |

## 4. Claims cut

- *Cut:* that Carlos Gracie created or refined any specific technique. No source supports it at the required standard.
- *Cut:* any characterisation of his motives for taking his brothers into the academy. BJJ Heroes reports a motive (removing them from an abusive father) on Reila Gracie's authority; a private family motive from a single family source is not stated here.
- *Cut:* the "possibly the single most important figure in Brazilian jiu-jitsu history" framing from the source. Ranking language.
- *Cut:* the claim that the Gracie Diet is "used successfully to this day". Unfalsifiable as written and unsourced.

## 5. Editorial notes

- The 1951 portrait shows him in a shirt and tie, not a gi. That is a useful counterweight to the standard iconography, and the alt text says so plainly.
- The entry deliberately separates "built the institution" from "invented the technique", because the sources support the first and not the second.
